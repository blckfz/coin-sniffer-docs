# Whale alerts

Whale alerts highlight unusual volume activity. The label describes a market pattern; it does not identify a trader, wallet or institution.

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Whale Detector | A volume anomaly to inspect |
| Whale Accumulation | An anomaly with bullish context |
| Whale Distribution | An anomaly with bearish context |

Accumulation and Distribution are analytical labels, not proof that a particular participant is buying or selling. High activity does not guarantee a subsequent breakout.

Choose available Whale presets in **Settings → Alert Rules** and review their charts through **Alert History**.
