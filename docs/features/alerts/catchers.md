# Catcher and contrarian alerts

The alert settings include **Surge 42**, **Knife Catcher** and **Capitulation Catcher** under **V4 Long-Bias Contrarian**.

These presets highlight sell-off conditions for long-bias review. A falling price can trigger attention without confirming that a bottom has formed. The alert name is not an instruction to buy a decline.

Check the individual toggles under **Settings → Alert Rules** rather than assuming these presets are enabled. Review any resulting event in the chart and alert timeline.

These alerts are separate from a [Dojo plan](/features/dojo); an alert alone does not supply a Dojo zone, entry, stop and three targets.
