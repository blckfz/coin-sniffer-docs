# Free account

Creating a Coin-Sniffer account is free and needs only an email address. Once you have confirmed the address, signing in opens the **free view**. No card is required and nothing is charged.

## What a free account includes

| Section | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| This week's sample | One zone that is still waiting for price, shown in full: zone, entry, stop, three targets and its chart. |
| Zones in play | Every other zone that is waiting or running: coin, timeframe, direction, R:R and rating. **Entry, stop and targets are hidden.** |
| The record | Every zone that has ended — target reached, stopped, or retired before entry — with the plan it published and how it ended. |
| Email | An optional daily email with the scan. Off unless you switch it on. |

## What it does not include

- Entry, stop and targets for the zones in play, other than the week's sample.
- The momentum alerts (Surge, Scout, Whale, Raid, Catcher).
- The live dashboard: market list, intraday charts, watchlist and alert history.
- **Backed by**, **Volume** and **Point of control**, which are Pro-only on every account.

These are part of a subscription. Select **Start 7-day free trial** in the free view to see the plans; see [Trial access](/billing/trial) for how the trial works.

## The weekly sample

The sample is chosen automatically, one per week, from zones that are still waiting for price. It is fixed for the week: it does not change when another zone publishes, and it stays the week's sample after price reaches it or it ends, with its outcome shown. A new one is chosen the following week. Early in a week there may be no sample yet.

A sample is one plan, shown so you can see what a subscription provides. It is not a recommendation, and it is recorded the same way as every other zone, whichever way it ends.

## The daily email

Tick **Email me the daily scan** when you sign up, or switch **Daily scan by email** on in the free view. It is sent on days when there is something to report, not every day.

To stop it, use the **unsubscribe** link at the bottom of any of the emails, or switch it off in your account. Unsubscribing does not change anything else about your account.

## If your subscription ends

An account whose trial or subscription has ended returns to the free view; it is not locked out. You can restart a plan from there at any time.

Read [Dojo Zones](/features/dojo) for the meaning and limitations of each status.
