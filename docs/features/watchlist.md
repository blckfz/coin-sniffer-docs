# Watchlist

Your watchlist keeps selected coins easy to find in **Market Coins** and **Alert History**.

## Add or remove a coin

Click the **star** beside a coin. Click it again to remove the coin. Starred coins are grouped above the other coins in the tables; there is no separate watchlist filter required for this grouping.

Your watchlist is saved to your account and is available when you sign in on another device. Browser display preferences are separate from the account watchlist.

## What a star changes

A star changes how you organise the covered markets. It does not add an unsupported symbol to coverage or guarantee that an alert or Dojo setup will appear for it.

A coin can remain saved after it leaves the current market list. A historical Dojo setup can also remain visible independently of the watchlist.

## Watchlist delivery

With [Pro webhooks](/features/webhooks), enable **Watchlist alerts only** on a destination to restrict its delivery to saved coins. Starring a coin by itself does not restrict all in-app notifications to the watchlist.
