# Watchlist

The watchlist lets you save specific coins and filter the screener and alert feed to show only those pairs.

## Adding coins to your watchlist

1. Find the coin in the screener table.
2. Click the **star (★) icon** on that row.
3. The coin is added to your watchlist immediately.

Alternatively, use the **Watchlist** panel if visible in the sidebar:
1. Click **+ Add coin**.
2. Type the symbol (e.g., ETHUSDT).
3. Select from the dropdown.

## Filtering by watchlist

Toggle the **Watchlist** switch in the filter bar above the screener table. The table will show only your starred coins.

The alert feed can also be filtered to show only watchlist alerts — look for the **Watchlist** filter toggle in the alert feed header.

## Removing coins

Click the ★ star icon again on any starred coin to remove it from your watchlist. Or use the Watchlist panel and click the × icon next to the coin.

## Watchlist persistence

Your watchlist is saved to your account and syncs across devices. If you log out and back in, your watchlist is restored.

## Watchlist alerts

Alert cards for watchlist coins are visually distinguished (coloured border or badge). When subscribing to webhooks, you can configure separate webhook URLs for main-screener alerts vs watchlist-only alerts — see [Webhooks](/features/webhooks).
