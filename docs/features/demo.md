# Public preview

The [Coin-Sniffer landing page](https://coin-sniffer.com) includes a preview using selected market charts and Dojo setups. You do not need an account to view it.

## What you can explore

Select a setup row to view its chart and the accompanying explanation. Historical setups made available in full show their zone, entry, stop, targets and recorded outcome.

Prices for active plans are hidden in the preview. A locked row or missing plan overlay is expected; full access is available in the signed-in app with an active subscription or eligible trial.

The preview covers only selected symbols. It is not the full market list or your personal watchlist.

## Read the record carefully

A setup published on a particular date becomes relevant from publication onward. Earlier candles crossing its levels are not evidence of a later entry.

**Invalid** means the setup ended before a recorded entry. **Stopped** means a stop outcome was recorded after entry. They should not be counted as the same event.

The preview's update label describes the preview refresh. It does not guarantee that every candle or price is current. For a frozen price or missing chart, see [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#charts-and-prices).

Read [Dojo Zones](/features/dojo) for the meaning and limitations of each status.
