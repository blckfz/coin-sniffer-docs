# Plans & access

Coin-Sniffer subscriptions are purchased and managed through Whop. The web app currently presents one screener subscription with monthly or yearly billing.

## Web app subscription

| Billing period | Price displayed in the app |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly | $39.99 per month |
| Yearly | $399.90 per year |

The subscription includes the market dashboard, built-in alerts, charts, watchlist and Dojo Zones. **Backed by**, **Volume** and **Point of control** — the fields describing how a zone was arrived at — are shown on Pro and admin accounts only. Check the final price, trial eligibility and renewal terms at checkout before confirming; the checkout terms apply to your purchase.

Start from [Coin-Sniffer pricing](https://coin-sniffer.com/#pricing), or follow the plan selection screen after signing in.

## Separate access

| Product or feature | Access |
| --- | --- |
| Public preview | Available without signing in; selected symbols and restricted active-plan details. |
| [Free account](/features/free-account) | An email address, no card. Zones in play without their prices, one zone a week in full, the record of ended zones, and an optional daily email. |
| TradingView Dojo indicators | Sold separately and invite-only. Check the indicator offer for included scripts and current pricing. |
| Community webhooks | Pro feature, negotiated and priced separately. Requires an active subscription and Pro enabled. |

A standard subscription or trial does not include community webhooks. A TradingView purchase does not automatically provide web app access. Existing indicator add-on holders may see their entitlement and username field on the Billing page.

## Getting access onto the right account

Use the **same email address in Whop and Coin-Sniffer**, and verify your Coin-Sniffer email. This also applies if you purchase before creating your app account.

For trials, read [Trial access](/billing/trial). For renewals, cancellation or payment issues, see [Manage subscription](/billing/manage).
