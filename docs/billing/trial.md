# Trial access

The app advertises a **seven-day trial** for eligible customers through Whop checkout. Registering a Coin-Sniffer account alone does not start the trial or unlock the dashboard; it opens the [free view](/features/free-account).

## Start a trial

1. Create and verify your [Coin-Sniffer account](/getting-started/signup).
2. In the free view, select **Start 7-day free trial** and choose a plan; this leads to Whop checkout.
3. Review whether a trial is offered, its duration, payment requirements and the date and amount of any first charge.
4. Complete checkout and return to the app.

If you start on Whop first, register and verify the same email in Coin-Sniffer afterward.

Do not assume that no payment method is required or that every purchase qualifies. The offer shown at checkout determines those terms.

## What it covers

An activated web app trial provides access to the screener, built-in alerts, charts, watchlist and Dojo Zones. It does not automatically include the separately sold TradingView scripts, Pro webhook delivery, or the Pro-only plan fields (**Backed by**, **Volume** and **Point of control**). Dojo plans themselves — zone, entry, stop, targets, R:R and the confluence rating — are included.

## Before it ends

Check your subscription details in [Billing](https://coin-sniffer.com/billing) and **Manage on Whop** for the renewal date and cancellation options. Do not rely on receiving a reminder email before a charge.

If you do not want to continue, cancel through Whop under the terms shown for your membership. If access expires, your account returns to the [free view](/features/free-account), where you can restart a plan. The [public preview](/features/demo) remains available without signing in.
