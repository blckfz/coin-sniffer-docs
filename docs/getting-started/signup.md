# Create an Account

## Sign up

1. Open [coin-sniffer.com](https://coin-sniffer.com) — you'll land on the home page.
2. Click **Start Free Trial**.
3. Enter your email and choose a password (min. 8 characters).
4. Click **Create Account**. You'll be signed in immediately.

No credit card is required to start. Your **7-day free trial** begins the moment you create your account.

## Free trial

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Duration | 7 days |
| Features | Full access to all screener & alert features |
| Webhooks | Available during trial |
| Charge | None until trial ends |

When the trial period ends, you'll see the **Subscription Expired** page. From there you can choose a plan and continue without losing any settings or watchlist data.

::: info
Trial accounts have full access — there are no feature restrictions. You get exactly the same experience as a paid subscriber.
:::

## Sign in (existing users)

1. Go to [coin-sniffer.com/login](https://coin-sniffer.com/login).
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click **Sign In**.

## Forgot your password?

1. Go to the login page and click **Forgot password?**
2. Enter your email address.
3. Check your inbox for a reset link (check spam if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes).
4. Click the link and set a new password.

## TradingView account access

If you have the **TradingView Indicators** plan or the **Bundle**, you'll also need to provide your TradingView username so your account can be granted access to the invite-only Pine scripts. See [Getting TradingView Access](/tradingview/getting-access) for details.
